# 07 — Executive Recommendation

**Deliverable:** `07_executive_recommendation.pptx` (or `.pdf`), **maximum 5 slides**.

Diagnosis · evidence · recommendation · 60-day plan · risks.

## The 60-day plan

No more than five actions. Each action states:

owner · expected business effect · risk · success metric · data needed for monitoring

## What separates a good deck here

- It names the stakeholder assumption the evidence **disproves or weakens**, and
  shows the evidence rather than asserting it.
- It distinguishes what the data supports from what it cannot conclude. Where
  evidence is insufficient, say so explicitly — that is in the brief.
- Effects are **sized**, not ranked. A driver with the worst rate is not
  automatically the driver with the biggest impact.
